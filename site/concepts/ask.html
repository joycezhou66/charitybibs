How this works

A charity bib is a guaranteed spot in the race. In return you promise to raise a set amount for the charity, usually $3,000 to $5,000, by a deadline. You give them a credit card when you're accepted. If you don't raise it all, they charge the rest to the card. That's normal. It's how the charity covers the spot it already paid for.

Every fact here was read from the charity's own page and links back to it. If it says "they don't say," we couldn't find it published. That's not a no. Ask. Any charity with a real program answers in one email. Keep the reply.
